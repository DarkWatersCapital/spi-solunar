Solunar periods, sun and moon are calculated on your phone. Tides and water temperature from NOAA CO-OPS; weather and waves from Open-Meteo. Data is saved so the app still works with weak signal.
The bite index blends moon periods, dawn and dusk, tide movement, barometer and wind. It is a planning guide, not a promise. Check the NWS marine forecast before running offshore.
