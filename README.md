# SPI Solunar

Solunar bite times, tides, weather and water temperature for South Padre Island and Port Isabel, Texas, on one phone-friendly page.

**Live app:** https://darkwaterscapital.github.io/spi-solunar/

## Install on your phone

- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

It then opens full screen from its own icon. Without signal it shows the last data it saved.

## Where the data comes from

- Solunar periods, sunrise, sunset and moon phase are calculated on the phone.
- Tides and water temperature: [NOAA CO-OPS](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/) stations 8779750 (Brazos Santiago Pass), 8779748 (SPI Coast Guard Station) and 8779770 (Port Isabel).
- Weather and waves: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), free for non-commercial use.

The bite index is a planning guide, not a promise. Check the NWS marine forecast before running offshore.
